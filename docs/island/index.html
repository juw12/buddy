Buddy Island

Buddy lives in the island at the top. Tap it to type, press and hold it to talk, or share a screenshot to Buddy from any app.

Keep it one tap away

  • iPhone / iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen. Then Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap (or the Action button) → a Shortcut that opens juw12.github.io/buddy/island/?talk=1.
  • Android: Menu → Install app. Then Share any screenshot → Buddy.
  • Computer: into a tiny window that stays on top of everything (Chrome, Edge). On Windows and Mac the full app also sits in your notch: get Buddy.
